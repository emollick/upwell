# Upwell · Car 07

A three-room passenger climber from the equatorial tether to Aster Reach, a rotating O’Neill habitat at the upper terminal. The ascent runs on a compressed timetable.

## Fabrication

Pearl ceramic-composite liners, graphite pressure spars and titanium hardware. Seat and sleep restraints, captive latches and tool tethers are fitted for changing apparent gravity. Wear is concentrated at touch points, guides and thresholds.

## Fixed assemblies

- Pelagic Anchor includes the retracting passenger bridge, gantries, cable winders, working crane, suspended rigging, crew launch, skiff, segmented pier, helipad, breakwater and marked buoy channel.
- The harbor fleet includes a fendered tug, a platform supply vessel with an open cargo deck, a twin-hull pilot launch and channel buoys. A floating cable workshop carries a gantry, lifting block, full and empty wire reels, mooring bitts and a glazed wheelhouse.
- The offshore cable depot stands on six splayed piles with open bracing. Its rescue helideck, crane pedestal and suspended lifting block, ship landing and communications masts give the anchorage a second fixed landmark.
- The receiving station has lead-in trusses, acquisition heads, lit pressure collars, underside work lights, capture arms, shutters, an operations gallery, control booths, a transfer bridge and the rotating habitat beyond its bearing.
- The cable assembly has a lenticular load core, round guide rails, helical auxiliary lines, splices and sprung wheel bogies. The opposite lane carries passing freight and passenger cars.
- Downbound climbers run on the same wheel mounts. Passenger cars have two glazed decks, freight cars have lift-out pallets and external canisters, and the inspection tender carries a cable reel, optical heads and folded probe arms on an open truss.

- Pressure-shell liners, carbon frames, seals, captive fasteners, cable raceways, return-air grilles, retention loops, light channels and airworthiness placards are fixed detailing within each room assembly.
- Exterior assemblies include the working tether and guide bogies, neighboring return ribbon and counterline carriages, Earth and atmosphere, and the rotating Aster Reach habitat with its nonrotating transfer hub.
